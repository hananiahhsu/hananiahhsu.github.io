<section class="content-section workflow-section">
  <div class="section-heading">
    <div>
      <p class="eyebrow">SOLUTION ARCHITECTURE</p>
      <h2>Algorithms become useful when the entire engineering path is designed.</h2>
    </div>
  </div>

  <div class="workflow-line">
    <article>
      <span>01</span>
      <h3>Interpret</h3>
      <p>Read CAD/BIM structures, engineering drawings, model annotations, material constraints, and enterprise rules.</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <span>02</span>
      <h3>Compute</h3>
      <p>Apply geometric processing, validation, comparison, optimization, classification, or parametric calculation.</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <span>03</span>
      <h3>Deliver</h3>
      <p>Produce review findings, nested layouts, release packages, calculation documents, architecture views, or quantities.</p>
    </article>
    <article>
      <span>04</span>
      <h3>Trace</h3>
      <p>Preserve clear relationships between engineering inputs, decisions, outputs, and subsequent changes.</p>
    </article>
  </div>
</section>
